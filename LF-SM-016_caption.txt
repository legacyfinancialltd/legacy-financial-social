Buildings insurance or contents insurance: do you need both?

Buildings insurance covers the structure of your home, such as the walls, roof and fitted kitchen. Most mortgage lenders require it to be in place from the day you exchange contracts.

Contents insurance covers your belongings, such as furniture, electronics and clothes.

Many people combine both in one policy. We can arrange cover that fits your home and your budget.
