Is your life insurance written in trust?

Many life insurance policies can be placed in trust. This can help the payout reach the people you choose more quickly, without waiting for probate, and may help with inheritance tax planning.

Insurers usually provide the forms and it is often simple to set up. We can check your existing policies and explain whether a trust could suit you.
