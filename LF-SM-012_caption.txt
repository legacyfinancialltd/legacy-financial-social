If illness or injury stopped you working, who would pay the mortgage?

Statutory sick pay and many employer schemes only last for a limited time. Income protection can pay a regular monthly income until you return to work or the policy ends, so you can keep up with your bills.

Policies vary in how long you wait before payments start and how long they last, so it is worth getting advice.
