What does working with Legacy Financial look like?

We start with a chat about your plans and your budget. Then we look at mortgages from a range of lenders, recommend the one that suits you and handle the application. We keep you updated and talk to your estate agent and solicitor through to completion.

We can also help with protection, home insurance and wills, so everything is in one place. Face to face in Barnsley, by phone or by video call.
