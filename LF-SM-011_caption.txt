Life insurance, critical illness cover and income protection: what is the difference?

Life insurance pays out if you die during the policy term.
Critical illness cover pays a lump sum if you are diagnosed with a serious illness covered by your policy.
Income protection pays a regular income if you cannot work because of illness or injury.

Many people use a mix of these to protect their family and their home. We will help you work out what matters most for you.
