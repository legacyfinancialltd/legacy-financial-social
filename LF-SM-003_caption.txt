What is an agreement in principle, and do you need one?

An agreement in principle (sometimes called a decision in principle) is a statement from a lender showing how much it may be willing to lend you, based on a quick look at your income and credit file. It is not a guaranteed mortgage offer, but it shows estate agents and sellers that you are serious.

We can arrange one with a lender that suits your circumstances, so you can view and make offers with confidence.
