Run a business? Here are three types of business protection worth knowing about.

Key person cover pays the business if someone it relies on dies or becomes seriously ill.
Shareholder protection can give the remaining owners the money to buy a deceased owner's shares.
Business loan protection can help repay business debts if a director dies or becomes seriously ill.
