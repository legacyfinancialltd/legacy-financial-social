Planning to apply for a mortgage? Check your credit file first.

Make sure you are on the electoral roll at your current address, check for mistakes or old accounts you no longer use, and try to avoid new credit applications in the months before you apply.

A tidy credit file will not guarantee a mortgage, but it gives you the best starting point. Worried about something on your file? Talk to us. Many lenders look at the full story.
