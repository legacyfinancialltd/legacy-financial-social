Getting ready to apply for a mortgage? Having your paperwork ready can help things move faster.

Most lenders will ask for:
• Photo ID and proof of address
• Your last three months of payslips (or accounts if you are self employed)
• Your latest P60
• Three months of bank statements
• Proof of your deposit

Your lender may ask for more depending on your circumstances. We will give you a personal checklist at your first appointment.
