Self employed and thinking about a mortgage? Here is what lenders usually look for.

Most lenders ask for two years of accounts or tax calculations (SA302s) with matching tax year overviews, and some will consider one year. Company directors may be assessed on salary and dividends, or on their share of company profit, depending on the lender.

Because every lender treats self employed income differently, advice really helps. We will match your income and paperwork to lenders whose criteria fit.
