Fixed or tracker: what is the difference?

A fixed rate stays the same for a set period, often two or five years, so your monthly payment does not change during that time.

A tracker rate follows the Bank of England base rate plus a set margin, so your payment can go up or down.

Neither is right for everyone. It depends on your budget, your plans and how comfortable you are with your payments changing. We will talk it through with you.
