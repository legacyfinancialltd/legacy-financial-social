Thinking about buying your first rental property?

Buy to let mortgages work differently from residential ones. Lenders usually look at the expected rent as well as your income, and deposits are often larger. You will also need to think about tax, insurance and your responsibilities as a landlord.

We can help you understand how much you may be able to borrow and what lenders will want to see. For tax questions, we recommend speaking to an accountant.

Book an appointment at legacyfinancial.co.uk or call 01226 643271.

Most buy to let mortgages are not regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.

Your home (or property) may be repossessed if you do not keep up repayments on your mortgage or any other debts secured on it. A fee may be charged for mortgage advice. The exact amount will depend on your circumstances.
