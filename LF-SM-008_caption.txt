What affects how much you can borrow?

Lenders look at more than your salary. They consider your regular outgoings, any loans or credit cards, childcare costs, your deposit and your credit history. They also check that you could still afford your payments if rates rose.

Small changes, such as clearing a credit card balance before you apply, can sometimes make a difference.
