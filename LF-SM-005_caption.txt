Moving home before your fixed rate ends? You may be able to take your mortgage with you.

This is called porting. It can help you avoid early repayment charges, but the new property and your circumstances still need to meet the lender's criteria, and any extra borrowing may be on a different rate. Sometimes a new mortgage works out better overall, even after charges.
