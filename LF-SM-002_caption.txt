Buying your first home? Here are five steps to get you started.

1. Check your credit file and fix any mistakes.
2. Work out your deposit and the extra costs, such as solicitor fees and surveys.
3. Get an agreement in principle so you know roughly what you could borrow.
4. Find a property and make your offer.
5. Apply for your mortgage, then work with your solicitor through to completion.
